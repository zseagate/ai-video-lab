## 1. 角色护照：小满

```
Xiaoman, a 24-year-old Chinese café barista, oval face, warm light skin,
dark brown eyes, short black bob hair with a soft side part, small silver
stud earring on the left ear, calm and slightly tired expression, gentle
half-smile. She wears a cream short-sleeve shirt, a rust-red canvas apron
with a small name tag, and dark trousers. Slim build, 7.5 head proportions,
natural posture. Flat vector illustration, clean geometric shapes, subtle
paper grain, cinematic warm-cool lighting, consistent character design.
```

**固定特征（不得改）**

- 短发黑色波波头，侧分
- 左耳小银钉
- 奶油色短袖衬衫
- 砖红色帆布围裙
- 深色长裤
- 不戴眼镜、不换发色、不换围裙颜色

```
A small corner café at 6:30 AM. Warm amber pendant lights, dark teal

a long walnut counter, a large window with cold blue morning light and a
faint city silhouette, light steam in the air, empty wooden chairs, quiet
and intimate atmosphere. Flat vector illustration, clean shapes, subtle
paper grain, cinematic lighting, 16:9 composition.
```

**固定特征**

- 深青色墙面

- 空气里有轻微蒸汽
